Family Law

Navigating Family Law Matters with Empathy and Expertise

When faced with relationship breakdowns, parenting arrangements, property settlements or divorce, our compassionate family law specialists step in to guide you through. We understand that these challenges can be emotionally draining, and that every situation is unique, so we approach each case with empathy and a focus on finding constructive resolutions that protect your best interests.

With our Principal Solicitor, Claire Nielsen, being an Accredited Specialist in Family Law, we can provide you with essential support in areas such as:

  • Navigating relationship breakdowns, including property settlements and parenting arrangements in marriages, de facto and same-sex relationships.
  • Divorces;
  • Crafting binding financial agreements that protect your interests (including before, during and after relationships);
  • Child Support issues; and
  • Spousal Maintenance issues.

Navigating the intricacies of family law requires a unique blend of skills and expertise. At Shipton & Associates, we pride ourselves on offering a comprehensive array of services, including:

  • A profound grasp of the ever-evolving family law landscape;
  • Training in mediation and Collaborative Law processes to promote harmonious resolutions.
  • A passion for facilitating amicable negotiated settlements, avoiding lengthy court proceedings wherever possible.
  • Up to date knowledge of Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia Practices and Procedures should litigation become necessary.
  • Superior advocacy and drafting skills to provide you with the best possible representation if Court proceedings are required.
  • A wealth of experience, drawing from diverse backgrounds and life journeys.
  • Expertise in property, trusts, wills and estate law and how these interact with family issues.
  • An understanding of the intricate and ever-changing challenges parents face as their children grow and develop.

With our expertise and compassionate approach, you can rest assured that your family law matter is in empathetic and capable hands.
