about us

Welcome to Shipton & Associates – not your typical law firm

Based on the iconic shores of Manly Beach in Sydney, our team of experienced lawyers and support staff are passionately dedicated to achieving positive outcomes for our clients on the Northern Beaches and beyond.

We are on a mission to create positive outcomes and provide practical, cost-effective solutions for our clients on the Northern Beaches and beyond.

We don't just provide legal advice; we offer workable, cost-effective solutions.

At Shipton & Associates, we are not your typical law firm; we pride ourselves on delivering modern, engaging and workable solutions tailored to your unique needs.

We believe in the power of listening and delivering sensible legal advice with clear explanations provided in plain English.

We speak your language

At Shipton & Associates, we understand that the legal world can be complex and overwhelming. That's why we're committed to cutting through the jargon and providing you with plain English explanations. Our approach is centred around you – your goals, your concerns, and your aspirations. From the moment you walk through our doors, we listen attentively, ensuring that your voice is heard and understood.

Navigating the Legal Landscape

Life is a journey, and at every stage, you deserve expert guidance to navigate the various terrains. Whether you are starting your adult life, acquiring assets, planning for a comfortable retirement or going through a relationship breakdown or mourning the loss of a loved one, we are here to assist you.

As a versatile legal practice, we cover a wide range of areas including family law, wills and estates and conveyancing.

Our expertise span across key areas, including family law, estate planning, deceased estates administration and conveyancing – which allows us to support you in various essential matters.

Working with us

At Shipton & Associates, we don't just practice law – we build lasting relationships.

Your peace of mind is our priority and our mission is to empower you with the knowledge and support you need to make informed decisions.

Experience the difference of working with a modern and engaging law firm that genuinely cares about your well-being and success.
